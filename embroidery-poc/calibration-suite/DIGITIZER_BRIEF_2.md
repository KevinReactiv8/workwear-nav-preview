# Digitizing brief — StitchDraft calibration suite, batch 2 (6 sheets)

Thanks for taking this on. These 6 sheets are test artwork — please
digitize them exactly as you would real customer work, using your normal
production judgement. Where something is too small or not sensible to
stitch, handle it as you normally would (skip it, thicken it, switch
technique) — and please jot a one-line note per sheet telling us what you
changed or skipped. Those notes are as valuable to us as the files.

## Sizes

- Digitize **every sheet at exactly 100 mm design width** (the artwork is
  drawn to scale — the mm labels on the sheets are true at 100 mm).
- Sheets **11 and 16 also at 250 mm width** (jacket-back scale) —
  8 stitch files in total.

## Ground rules

- **Grey text and numbers are labels — do not stitch them.** Stitch only
  the navy/red/gold/white artwork.
- Please **don't redraw or tidy the artwork** — digitize what's there.
- On sheet 11 the white text/star inside the coloured bands is a
  **knockout** — treat the white as unstitched fabric or stitched white,
  whichever you'd normally do, and note which you chose.
- Sheets 13 and 14 are deliberately awkward (gradients, halftone,
  distress). Handle them as you would a real customer supplying that art
  — blends, banding, simplification or a "needs redraw" note are all
  acceptable answers. What you'd *actually do* is the data we want.
- Assume **cotton twill / polo piqué**, standard 40wt rayon or poly thread.
- Colours are indicative; pick sensible thread shades.

## Deliverables per sheet

- .DST (essential)
- Your native file (.EMB or equivalent) and the production PDF if easy
- File naming: `11_knockout_text_100mm.dst` etc.
- Your notes: anything skipped, thickened, or handled specially
