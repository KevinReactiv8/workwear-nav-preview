How it works

From artwork to approved stitch file in five steps.

Ingest backlog

Your historical stitch files are mined into a measured style profile: densities, satin widths, underlay habits, trim discipline.

Job arrives

Customer artwork (PNG, JPG, vector, PDF) is vectorized, colour-separated and classified shape-by-shape: satin column, tatami fill, fine-detail run.

Three files, seconds later

A production .DST in your measured parameters — underlay, pull compensation, lock stitches included — plus the artwork as clean true-size .SVG vectors and a sewn preview.

Expert review, two routes

Approve as-is and the .DST goes straight to the machine. Needs work? Import the .SVG into Wilcom and punch native objects over finished art prep — a clean first-generation .EMB, no stitch conversion.
