How it works

From artwork to approved stitch file in five steps.

Ingest backlog

Your historical stitch files are mined into a measured style profile: densities, satin widths, underlay habits, trim discipline.

Job arrives

Customer artwork (PNG, JPG, vector, PDF) is vectorized and classified shape-by-shape: satin column, tatami fill, fine-detail run.

Draft in your style

The engine generates a production .DST using your measured parameters — underlay, pull compensation, lock stitches included.

Expert review

Your digitizer sees a sewn-preview in the browser, approves or fixes the hard 20%. Minutes, not most of an hour.

Flywheel

The difference between draft and final is captured as training signal. The next draft needs less fixing.
